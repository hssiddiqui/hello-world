# Analytical Validation Exercise
## Objective
You are provided a dataset containing **loss outputs from a next‑generation catastrophe risk model**. Determine whether it is **fit for client use**, based on the standards you believe are appropriate for a **production Verisk model**.
## Deliverable
Submit a **concise analytical report** that describes your **validation approach**, highlights **key data issues or limitations**, provides a **clear recommendation** (approve, reject, or approve with conditions), supported by evidence
## Submission
Strong submissions are **reproducible**, present a **clear analytical narrative**, and base conclusions on **rigorous validation**.  
Submissions may be reviewed prior to the interview and will be used as the basis for discussion.
You may use any tools you deem appropriate, including code, documentation, or LLM tools (within the confidentiality constraints explained in the email).
## Timeframe
**~3 calendar days from reciving this question package**

*Suggested effort: 2–3 hours. If you run out of time, briefly list the additional tests or analyses you would perform with more time.*
